# Lesson 9 — short phrases (2–5 words)

Vocabulary: Knudsvig chapters 1–9 (dataset `knudsvig-731`). Grammar through Lesson 9:
3rd-person verbs only, present and perfect, active and passive; complementary infinitive;
ablative with and without prepositions (means, manner, time, personal agent with *ā/ab*);
singular and plural; *is ea id* as pronoun and adjective; cardinal numbers. No accusative
with prepositions, dative, genitive, or 1st/2nd person yet.

The categories are mixed, so students have to work out what kind of phrase each one is.
The English key gives the category.

---

## Lesson 9

### Latin

1. **nātūra dīvīna**
2. **ā Caesare victī sunt**
3. **is vīcit**
4. **ex castrīs**
5. **canibus impedītus est**
6. **aciem īnstrūxit**
7. **magnā cum cūrā**
8. **id factum est**
9. **fugere nōn potuit**
10. **cōpiae ā Caesare īnstrūctae sunt**
11. **multīs lacrimīs**
12. **eae fūgērunt**
13. **prō Caesare**
14. **lacrimīs victa est**
15. **eā nocte dormīvit**
16. **decem aprōs cēpit**
17. **ab amīcīs adiūtus est**
18. **in harēnā periit**
19. **ālea iacta est**
20. **quis eam docuit?**

### English

1. divine nature *(noun + adj.: f. nom. sg.)*
2. they were conquered by Caesar *(pf. passive + personal agent)*
3. he conquered *(pronoun + verb)*
4. out of the camp *(prep. phrase)*
5. he was hindered by the dogs *(pf. passive + means)*
6. he drew up the battle line *(verb + direct object)*
7. with great care *(abl. of manner, adj. + cum + noun)*
8. it was done *(pronoun + pf. passive)*
9. he was not able to flee *(complementary inf.)*
10. the troops were drawn up by Caesar *(pf. passive + personal agent)*
11. with many tears *(noun + adj.: abl. pl.)*
12. they (the women) fled *(pronoun + verb: f. pl.)*
13. in front of Caesar / on behalf of Caesar *(prep. phrase)*
14. she was overcome by tears *(pf. passive + means)*
15. he slept that night *(abl. of time + verb, is as adj.)*
16. he captured ten boars *(numeral + noun + verb)*
17. he was helped by his friends *(pf. passive + personal agent)*
18. he died in the arena *(prep. phrase + verb)*
19. the die has been cast *(subject + pf. passive)*
20. who taught her? *(question + pronoun object)*
